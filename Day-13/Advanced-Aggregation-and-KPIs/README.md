# Day 13: Advanced Aggregation and KPI Calculation

## 🎯 Objective
This session focused on advanced SQL techniques to derive meaningful business insights. I worked on grouping data, filtering aggregated results, and calculating Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) to analyze e-commerce trends.

## 🛠️ Skills Demonstrated
- **Advanced Grouping:** Utilized `GROUP BY` to categorize data across multiple dimensions (Category, Date, Employee).
- **Conditional Filtering:** Applied `HAVING` clause to filter aggregated data (e.g., finding segments with specific revenue thresholds).
- **KPI Generation:** Calculated Total Revenue, Order Volume, and Average Pricing per category.
- **Time-Series Analysis:** Extracted temporal trends by grouping records by `YEAR()` and `orderDate`.
- **Ranking & Sorting:** Used `ORDER BY` and `LIMIT` to identify top performers.

## 📈 Business Insights Extracted
- **Category Analysis:** Calculated total products and average price points for every product category.
- **Performance Metrics:** Evaluated employee efficiency based on order processing volume.
- **Customer Behavior:** Analyzed spending patterns and identified high-frequency customer segments.
- **Trend Spotting:** Identified total revenue and order volume per date and year.
